How to play

Daily Mode

Empty the board. Ranked by fewest cats left, then by time.

Drag pieces from the tray onto the grid.
Three of a kind touching clear. So does a full row or column.
Every cat counts, yours too. It ends when the board's empty, the pieces run out, or nothing fits.
See the whole deck. The last ten pieces are small — plan the finish.
Every 30 cats cleared adds a fortune cookie (clears 3×3) or a red packet (clears its row and column) to your deck. Drag it anywhere, even on top of cats.
∞Unlimited Cat Mode — collect as many cats as you can, until you can't move any more. Top scores are ranked on most cats.

About Lucky Streak

Life is made of little moments worth noticing.

A good game. A curious discovery. A ridiculous cat. A perfect little streak of things going right.

Lucky Streak is a celebration of those moments — and a reminder to be grateful for them.

Play, notice, enjoy.

There's a lot of good hiding in the little things.
